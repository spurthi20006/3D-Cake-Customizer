# 🍰 Day 4 – Realistic Toppings & Fondant Customization

**Project:** WHISKORA  
**Tagline:** Whisk Your Imagination to Life.

## 🎯 Today's Goal

To introduce realistic cake decorations, toppings and fondant customization, making the cake designing experience more creative and visually appealing.

## 🍓 Planned Development

### 1. Realistic Cake Toppings
Introduce a collection of realistic cake decorations:

- Fresh strawberries with natural skin texture and tiny seeds.
- Blueberries with realistic colour and surface details.
- Glossy red cherries.
- Chocolate curls and chocolate pieces.
- Oreo cookies.
- Macarons.
- Edible flowers.

### 2. Fondant Customization

Introduce decorative fondant elements:

- Fondant flowers.
- Fondant bows.
- Hearts and stars.
- Cute fondant characters.
- Custom decorative shapes.
- Multiple fondant colour options.

### 3. Interactive Customization

- Allow users to select multiple toppings.
- Display selected decorations on the cake preview.
- Allow users to remove unwanted decorations.
- Provide different fondant colour choices.
- Update the cake preview whenever customization options change.

### 4. Realistic Visual Experience

- Move beyond basic emoji and flat CSS decorations.
- Explore realistic fruit textures and 3D models.
- Investigate Three.js for interactive 3D cake visualization.
- Focus on natural-looking details, lighting and textures.

## 🛠️ Technologies to Explore

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript
- Three.js
- Realistic 3D models and textures

## 🚀 Expected Outcome

A more interactive cake customization experience where users can decorate their cakes with realistic fruits, toppings and fondant elements.

## 💡 Today's Takeaway

The goal is to make cake customization feel more like designing a real cake, giving users the freedom to bring their imagination to life.

**Project Status:** Realistic Toppings & Fondant Customization – Planned.
