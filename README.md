# 🍰 Something Sweet Is in the Making!

### What if you could design your dream cake instead of just choosing one?

That's the idea behind my new project – **3D Cake Customizer!**

Imagine having the freedom to create a cake exactly the way you want it. Choose your favourite flavour, experiment with colours, select beautiful decorations, add personalized messages, and bring your imagination to life!

### ✨ What's Coming?

- 🎂 Customize cakes with different flavours, shapes, and sizes.
- 🎨 Experiment with frosting colours and creative finishes.
- 🍓 Decorate cakes with fruits, chocolates, nuts, and more.
- 💌 Add personalized messages and occasion tags.
- 🎁 Choose additional decorations and accessories.
- 🔄 Explore your creation through an interactive 3D preview.
- 🖼️ Discover inspiration from a gallery of customized cakes.

And that's not all! I'm also working towards creating a dedicated bakery management section to make the ordering and management experience smoother.

### 💭 The Vision

The idea is simple: **Don't just order a cake. Create one!**

I want to make cake customization more interactive, enjoyable, and personal, giving everyone the freedom to turn their cake ideas into something special.

### 🚀 The Journey Begins!

This is just the beginning. I'll be documenting my progress, sharing new features, improvements, and challenges throughout the development journey.

**Something sweet is on its way. Stay tuned! ❤️🍰**
