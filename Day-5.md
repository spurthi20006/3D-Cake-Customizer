# 🎂 Day 5 – Introducing 3D Cake Customization

**Project: WHISKORA**  
**Tagline: Whisk Your Imagination to Life.**

## 🎯 Today's Goal

The goal of Day 5 is to take WHISKORA beyond basic cake customization and introduce an interactive 3D cake-designing experience.

Inspired by realistic 3D cake designs, the plan is to allow users to customize different parts of a cake and see their selections reflected in a live preview.

## ✨ Planned Features

### 1. Cake Shape Customization
- Round cakes
- Heart-shaped cakes
- Square cakes

### 2. Cake Size
- 500g
- 1kg
- 2kg

### 3. Cake Layers
- Single-layer cake
- Double-layer cake
- Triple-layer cake

### 4. Sponge Customization
- Vanilla
- Chocolate
- Red Velvet

### 5. Cream & Filling
- Different cream flavours
- Customizable filling colours
- Visible cream layers

### 6. Frosting Customization
- Multiple frosting colours
- Smooth frosting
- Textured frosting
- Layered frosting designs

### 7. Cake Decorations
- Cream swirls
- Fruits and toppings
- Decorative elements
- Custom cake-top designs

### 8. Interactive 3D Preview
- Rotate the cake.
- Zoom in and out.
- View the cake from different angles.
- See customization changes instantly.

## 🛠️ Technology Planned

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript
- Three.js – 3D graphics library

## 🎨 Design Direction

The 3D cake preview will follow a polished, stylized appearance inspired by the reference design.

The objective is to make the customization experience visually appealing, interactive and easy to use.

## 🚀 Expected Outcome

A functional 3D cake customizer where users can experiment with cake shapes, sizes, layers, flavours, frosting and decorations through an interactive preview.

## 📌 Current Status

**3D Cake Customization – Planned for Development.**

The implementation and feature testing will be documented as development progresses.
