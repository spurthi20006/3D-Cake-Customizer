Good to know

Questions, answered.

How far in advance should I order?

For custom cakes, we recommend placing your order at least 3 days ahead. For larger celebrations or intricate finishes, a week gives our kitchen the time to make every detail just right.

Do you offer eggless cakes?

Yes. Eggless vanilla is one of our five core flavours, and our team takes care to keep your preferences clear throughout the order.

Where do you deliver?

We currently deliver across Bengaluru. Delivery slots and fees are confirmed with your order details, so your cake arrives as beautifully as it left our kitchen.
