## ✨ Live Features Deployed

- **Interactive 3D Cake Studio (Three.js):** Drag to rotate, scroll/buttons to zoom, `3/4 View`, `Top`, `Side`, `Front`, `Auto-rotate`, `Slice view` cutaway, and `Reset`.
- **Full Cake Customization:**
  - Sponge flavours (`Vanilla`, `Eggless Vanilla`, `Chocolate`, `Red Velvet`, `Butterscotch`)
  - Shapes (`Round`, `Heart`, `Square`), sizes (`500 g`, `1 kg`, `2 kg`), and layers (`Single`, `Double`, `Triple`)
  - Cream flavours & customizable filling colours
  - Frosting styles (`Smooth`, `Textured`, `Layered`) and 8 buttercream shades
  - 3D decorations (`Cream swirls`, `Strawberries`, `Blueberries`, `Cherries`, `Chocolate curls`, `Cookie pieces`, `Macarons`, `Edible flowers`, `Gold pearls`, `Pink drip`, `Chocolate drip`, `Rainbow sprinkles`)
  - Fondant toppers (`Flower`, `Bow`, `Heart`, `Star`), custom cake-top message plaque, and animated 3D taper candles
- **Inspiration Gallery → 3D Plate:** Clicking any inspiration cake (`Strawberry cloud`, `Midnight`, `Petal pink`) loads it directly onto the 3D plate for further customization.
- **Export & Inquiry Tools:** `Save PNG` 3D snapshot export, `#design=` shareable URL links, `localStorage` auto-save, and the `Request this cake` order inquiry modal.
